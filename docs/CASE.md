# Our case: Case 2, Alternative-Format Study Tool

*Transcribed from the 2026 Ivy Tech IT Challenge team handout (Data Analytics, Applied AI & Informatics track).*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Persona (who it serves)** | Online student who studies late at night on a phone and speaks English as a second language |
| **Problem worth solving** | Course materials come in one format, and tutoring is closed when she studies |
| **Seed idea** | Tool that turns course content into study summaries and practice questions. A starting point: we may sharpen or change the solution as long as it serves this persona and problem |
| **Strategic Plan 2030** | Pillar: **Academic & Instructional Quality**. Statewide strategy: **Ensure Consistent Quality Across Learning Modalities** |
| **A-Ivy focus area** | Learning and study support. A-Ivy is Ivy Tech's statewide plan for responsible AI |
| **Seed 2030 metric** | Online course completion rate. Choose at least one measurable metric the solution would move by 2030 |
| **Starter user story** | "As an online student, I can turn course material into formats I can study at any hour so that I keep up with my class." |
| **A working prototype** | Accepts a course reading or set of lecture notes · Produces a summary of the material · Generates practice questions with answers |
| **Sample data to use** | Sample course readings or lecture notes. Use the provided dataset and made-up sample data only. Never use real student or employee information |

## Part 3 Roadblocks: pilot feedback (handout, team copy)
Pillar 1: Academic & Instructional Quality · Case 2: Alternative-Format Study Tool

| Finding | Pilot user said |
|---|---|
| **Finding 1** | "The summaries are still difficult to understand. Let me choose a simpler reading level or another language." |
| **Finding 2** | "I cannot tell whether the practice questions are based on the material my instructor actually assigned." |
| **Finding 3** | "When I am still stuck, I need an easy way to find a tutor or ask a real person for help." |

**Roadblock completion check:** in the final demo, the team must identify the two findings addressed, show the
changes working, and explain how IBM Bob helped implement the iteration. *We are addressing all three.*
