# Strategic Plan 2030 research: "The Future of College"

Sources: Ivy Tech newsroom, *New Strategic Plan Launched* (Jul 1, 2026); the full statewide plan is at
<https://www.ivytech.edu/strategicplan>. **Verify pillar goals and targets there before quoting them.**

## Facts we can cite
- Plan launched July 2026 and runs through 2030. Each of the **19 campuses** sets its own goals under the four pillars.
- Record headcount: **215,000+** students in 2025-26.
- Certificates, degrees and certifications awarded are **up 27%+** since the start of the decade.
- **4 in 5** graduates leave with no student debt and stay in Indiana; Ivy Tech adds **$4.8B** a year to Indiana's economy.
- Metric families the college monitors: **enrollment and retention, completions, wages after completion,
  educational advancement (transfer)**. Use these words for our outcome metric.

## The four pillars, with idea starters (choose on the day from the real data)
### 1. Academic & Instructional Quality
High-quality programs held to rigorous standards (e.g. full ATMAE accreditation).
- *Faculty:* early-alert dashboard. Which course sections show DFW (D/F/withdraw) spikes, and what predicts them?
- *Student:* AI study coach that turns a syllabus into a weekly plan with checkpoints.
- Metrics: course success rate, DFW rate, gateway-course pass rate.

### 2. Career & Transfer Pathways
Transparent roadmaps connecting every credential to a 4-year transfer or a high-demand career; apprenticeships
expanding into education, healthcare and community service.
- *Student:* pathway navigator. "With my credits, what's the fastest route to job X or transfer to school Y, and what does it pay?"
- *Employer / adviser:* skills-gap match between program outcomes and regional job postings.
- Metrics: completion, transfer rate, wages after completion, credits-to-degree.

### 3. Student-Centered Journey
Removing barriers through personalised, wraparound services.
- *Adviser:* at-risk prioritisation (who to call this week and why), using `ml.train` + permutation importance.
- *Student:* "one front door" assistant that routes basic-needs, financial-aid and tutoring questions.
- Metrics: fall-to-fall retention, persistence, time to first contact, service uptake.

### 4. Innovation & Future Readiness
Integrating AI into all programs.
- *Instructor:* AI-readiness map of programs, with suggested AI-skill modules per course.
- *Student:* AI-skills micro-credential tracker tied to employer demand.
- Metrics: share of programs with AI content, AI-credential completions, employer satisfaction.

## Guardrails that make us credible

humans stay in the loop (adviser decides, model ranks) · works with data Ivy Tech already collects.
