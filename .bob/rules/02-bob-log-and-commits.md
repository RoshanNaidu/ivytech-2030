# Logging and commit rules (all modes)

The challenge requires "a running log of how your team worked with IBM Bob", and judges follow commit history.

## After every meaningful task, append ONE row to `docs/BOB_LOG.md`
Columns: `| Time | Phase | Member | Bob mode | What we asked | What Bob produced | What we kept / changed and why |`
- Time as HH:MM local. Phase 1-4. Member = the teammate driving this Bob session (ask if unknown).
- Be specific and honest, including what was wrong and how we corrected it. That is what judges want to see.

## Suggest a commit after each working increment
Format: `P<phase>: <imperative summary>` e.g. `P2: add at-risk ranking to Predict page`.
Small, frequent commits from every member's own machine and GitHub account.
